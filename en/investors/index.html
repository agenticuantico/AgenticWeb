Let’s build the next generation of AI agents for Spanish-speaking markets and beyond

AgentiCuántico is an AI agents platform that talks by voice and text, plans tasks and carries them out with tools, always with human approval for anything that matters. We are looking for investors and partner companies in the United States, Spain, Switzerland, Italy, Argentina, Mexico, Colombia and worldwide.

Value proposition

Agents that finish the job

They don’t just answer: they plan, use tools, involve specialists and deliver verifiable results.

Natural voice and avatar

A warm conversational experience in seven languages, built for people who don’t want to learn new software.

Control and trust

Every consequential action needs explicit approval. No stored cards, and users stay in control of their data.

Market

We focus on professionals, SMBs and enterprise teams in Spanish- and Portuguese-speaking markets, where digital adoption is high and AI agents designed in their language and with local pricing are scarce. The same technical foundation scales to English, Italian, French and German-speaking markets.

Product

Agents

“Does it all for you” mode, invitable specialist agents (coding, research, design, legal, cybersecurity) and document analysis.

Voice companion

Hands-free calls with optional memory controlled by the user.

Automation

Recurring tasks and connectors with step-by-step approval on serverless infrastructure.
