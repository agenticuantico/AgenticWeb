Investment and partnerships

AgentiCuántico is an AI agent platform that talks by voice and text, plans tasks and carries them out with tools, always with human confirmation for anything important. We are looking for investment and partner companies, in Argentina and abroad.

Invest Partner with us Book a meeting

Value proposition

Multi-step tasks

The agent draws up a plan, uses tools (including a cloud browser) and brings in specialists; the person reviews the result.

Voice in seven languages

You can speak to it and it answers out loud, in Spanish and six other languages.

Confirmation before acting

Sending, publishing or paying requires the person’s approval. We do not store cards, and memory can be deleted.

Market

We serve professionals, SMBs and company teams in Spanish- and Portuguese-speaking markets, with prices in local currency. The website and the app are already translated into English and Portuguese, and this page is available in six languages.

Product

Agents

Multi-step tasks, a virtual computer (a cloud browser with a live screen), invitable specialist agents (coding, research, design, legal, cybersecurity) and document analysis.

Voice call with AgentiCuántico

Hands-free calls with optional memory controlled by the person.

Scheduled tasks and connections

Daily or weekly tasks (job search, web digest). GitHub and LinkedIn connections in testing.

Business model

Monthly subscription with no commitment, billed in Argentine pesos through Mercado Pago or in US dollars through PayPal.
